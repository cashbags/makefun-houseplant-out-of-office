## The line

> Out of office. I have leaned toward the window and I am not coming back. Forward all mail to the sun.

101 characters. The plant has leaned toward the window (phototropism) and treats the sun as its new office — its own biology is the joke's engine, and the line ends on its strongest beat, "Forward all mail to the sun". The lane was empty in the live field: all 665 visible entries were scanned and none used leaning toward the light or forwarding mail to the sun, while the field is dominated by the neglected fern, "ignored since March", unionising and spite.

The line won a ten-candidate round-robin tournament judged on the brief's own criteria in order (auto-reply form, surprise, plant life driving the joke, tightness, originality), 9–0. Full record: `CANDIDATES.md` in the build notes; winner research: `WINNER-RESEARCH.md`.
